# Bundled adhan recordings

The audio files below have their own content licenses, independent of the application's source-code license. Attribution, provenance, alterations and source/license URLs are also bundled in `src/features/prayer/sounds.data.json` and shown offline in each recording's information section. Recording credits identify the recordist; they do not assert an undocumented muezzin identity or endorsement.

These licenses allow copying and redistribution under their stated terms. The two share-alike audio adaptations retain their respective share-alike licenses. No added DRM or additional restriction applies to these audio files. The regional labels describe the documented recording locations, not a certification of a regional melody or performer. The short reading has no invented regional label.

Verified source and packaged-byte hashes, measured duration and regeneration details are in `docs/implementation/android-polish/bundled-adhans/`. Original downloaded recordings and private app backups are not committed.
