# iPhone fixes and Quran navigation validation

## Result

Before the subsequent offline-bundling change, the build was installed in place on the owner's iPhone 15. The owner
confirmed the reported fixes and then confirmed that the new Quran navigation
works well with both Hafs and Warsh. Physical-device confirmation is separate
from the automated and simulator checks below.

## Confirmed behavior

- Hafs renders the unchanged King Fahd Complex Arabic source with its original
  paired font. Source openings stay outside the first ayah, with Al-Fatihah's
  counted basmala retained as ayah 1 and no opening added to At-Tawbah.
- Both mushafs display their original verse markers once. Single-ayah audio
  works in continuous reading, the mini-player closes, and the reader remains
  responsive. Cached playback no longer flashes a download/preparation sheet.
- Completed adhkar and dua cards use the completion background. Saved counter
  identities were not reset or migrated.
- Western prayer-time digits are unclipped and the Qibla/adhan shortcut heights
  match on iPhone.
- The compact Quran index offers السور، الأجزاء، والأحزاب for either riwaya.
  The reader title opens an in-screen picker with direct `surah:ayah` jumps.
  Selecting a juz/hizb retains that section when continuing across surahs and
  respects its final ayah. The owner checked the first-hizb continuation from
  Al-Fatihah into Al-Baqarah.

## Automated and simulator validation

- 187 domain/UI regression tests pass, including complete, nonoverlapping
  coverage of 114 surahs, 30 juz and 60 hizbs in each riwaya; scoped restoration
  and continuation; source preservation; and cached audio preparation.
- `yarn quality` passes: JavaScript/TypeScript and Python lint, formatting,
  native style checks, and TypeScript checking.
- Seven native iOS tests pass, including bounded verse export/cache reuse,
  invalid audio ranges and paths, and original Warsh archive/font checks.
- Release simulator and signed physical-iPhone builds succeed. Simulator
  checks include mid-surah section openings without an inserted basmala,
  independent Warsh hizb starts, a direct jump to Hafs 2:255, and a picker that
  remains usable above the keyboard and hides underlying accessibility controls.
- Source and boundary verification commands:

```sh
python3 tools/phase5/catalog.py --check
python3 tools/phase5/verify-source.py
python3 tools/quran-navigation/verify.py --check
python3 tools/offline-quran/align.py --check
```

Boundary references, pinned revisions, licenses, verification rules and the two
Hafs publisher `jozz` label discrepancies are documented in
[the index verification notes](../tools/quran-navigation/README.md).

## Data and release boundaries

The in-place installation preserved all 207 counters, nine bookmarks, three
reminders, existing downloaded packs and audio timing records. Reader positions
advance during testing and now retain section context; a separate record stores
the selected index tab. Settings values were preserved apart from their update
timestamp.

At this validation checkpoint, Android retained its existing Hafs text descriptor and surah navigation. The subsequent offline-bundling change shares the new Hafs source across platforms; its separate validation is recorded in [offline mushaf QA](OFFLINE_MUSHAF_QA_2026-10-21.md). Shared
completion styling and HTTP header normalization remain shared code. This work
does not update an Android device/store release, upload an iPhone binary, or
resubmit the App Store review.
