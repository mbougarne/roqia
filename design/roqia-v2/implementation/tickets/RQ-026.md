## Phase 9 delivery — 18 October 2026

The normal catalog now delivers both pinned Tanzil originals directly over HTTPS, with separate installs and deterministic on-device import. All 114 chapters / 6236 verses per language are checked against source IDs and expected derived hashes. Original strings, source footers, translator names and non-commercial terms are retained. English is Pickthall. No translated text is bundled in the app or republished by the repository.

Hafs selections persist offline; Arabic remains readable if the selected translation is missing or corrupt. The reader offers translation management/re-download. The catalog, import and runtime reject wrong edition, text version, chapter, duplicate/missing verses and unreviewed Warsh joins. Arabic source/font assets are unchanged.

See [phase 9 source and Android evidence](../../../../docs/implementation/phase-9/README.md). This supersedes phase 4's unavailable normal-catalog/publication status. Delivery is authorized for this free non-commercial app; no additional owner approval gate is introduced. Qualified editorial sample review is not claimed. Full TalkBack listening, older/physical Android acceptance and the later visual pass remain tracked; iOS testing is deferred. Status stays `in_progress` for those outstanding acceptance checks, not because downloading is blocked.
